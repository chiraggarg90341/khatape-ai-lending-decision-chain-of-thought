Task 4 – Direct Answer on a Simple Question

Objective

To test whether AI can answer a simple one-step question using direct prompting.

Tool and Date

* Tool: ChatGPT
* Date: 23 November 2026

Exact Prompt

Here are 8 lending scenarios for KhataPe, a digital lender.

For each scenario, which option has the higher interest rate? Answer only A or B.

Output a table: ID | Higher interest rate

Scenarios: [My 8 scenarios from 02_scenarios.md]

AI Output

ID	Higher interest rate
S01	A
S02	B
S03	A
S04	B
S05	B
S06	A
S07	B
S08	A

Verification

I compared the AI output with my independently checked answers.

* Total scenarios: 8
* Correct answers: 8
* Incorrect answers: 0
* Accuracy: 100%

Error Found

No errors found. All eight answers correctly identified the option with the higher interest rate.

Observation

The task required only comparing two interest-rate values. It did not require profit calculations or applying the tie rule.

Conclusion

Direct prompting worked correctly for this simple one-step task. It gave all eight correct answers in a clear table. This shows that simple questions do not always require step-by-step prompting.
